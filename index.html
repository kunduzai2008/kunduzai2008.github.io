Sahil Ai

Sahil Ai is the official companion app of the Sahil Ai YouTube channel. In the app, viewers can watch the channel's videos and playlists, open the channel's important links and services, read announcements from the channel owner, and chat with the owner.

Before a video plays, the app asks the viewer to sign in with Google and checks whether that Google account is subscribed to the Sahil Ai YouTube channel. Videos are for subscribers of the channel.
